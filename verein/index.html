Adjustments to Nothing Verein

Adjustments to Nothing Verein is a non-commercial association based in Zurich, Switzerland.

The association creates, develops, publishes, and curates artistic and cultural projects at the intersection of sound, time, memory, and perception.

It supports experimental artistic practices, research-based works, and digital tools that explore listening, presence, and non-utilitarian forms of experience.

Its activities may include the development and publication of software applications, the organisation of artistic events, and the production of related materials.
